Nereon: raw RL first, then public-information planning

The combined candidate improves Nereon's observed four-player win share from
12.17% to 16.54%, but Nereon remains the weakest member of this table. This is
an initial PPO pilot, not evidence that raw RL has reached its ceiling.

Leader       Original Nereon    Retrained Nereon    Retrained + planner
Melia              26.38%             25.88%               26.71%
Nereon             12.17%             13.50%               16.54%
Thaleia            39.92%             38.33%               35.71%
Doreios            21.54%             22.29%               21.04%

Nereon paired changes, in percentage points (95% intervals):
  Raw RL versus original:       +1.33  [-0.17, +2.84]
  Planner versus retrained:     +3.04  [-0.54, +6.62]
  Combined versus original:     +4.38  [+0.67, +8.08]

The combined interval excludes zero on this benchmark. The individual raw-RL
and planner increments remain uncertain. The largest observed opposing shift
is Thaleia: -4.21 points versus the original table, interval [-8.07, -0.34].

Design
  400 matched games per column: 100 seed blocks, each with four seat rotations.
  Tied wins are split; intervals cluster the four rotations within each block.
  Melia and the Doreios/Thaleia planners stay fixed in every arm. Their internal
  models of opponents also stay frozen. Only Nereon's actual policy changes.
  The original column reuses the prior Thaleia study's completed games; 16 were
  reproduced with identical outcomes and planning counts. These benchmark seeds
  were excluded from this training and screening but are not fresh across the
  overall research program.

Training
  1,024 complete games, 62,982 Nereon decisions, 16 PPO updates/checkpoints.
  Terminal reward only: 2 * split-win credit - 1. No strategic overrides.
  Architecture unchanged: 171 inputs, two 128-unit hidden layers, 349 actions.
  A separate 128-game screen compared the original, 512-game checkpoint, and
  1,024-game checkpoint. The selected checkpoint was round-08 (512 games),
  selected before inspecting comparison outcomes. All checkpoints are saved.
  The planner uses that same selected actor and its matching signed-win critic:
  16 sampled worlds, up to four candidates, four-own-turn horizon, risk 1.
  Hypothetical continuations use plain neural actors, without nested planning.

Validation and recovery
  All 800 new comparison games passed exact full-state replay checks.
  Training/screening records also passed replay and integrity checks.
  Exported actor commands matched PyTorch on 930 decisions across 16 games.
  Critic tensors were exported losslessly; maximum floating-point kernel
  difference versus PyTorch was 9.5367431640625e-7, reported explicitly.
  The independent audit verified inputs, seed separation, all scheduled games,
  frozen opponent configurations, and independently recomputed paired statistics.
  Full results: results.json. Audit: final-audit.json. Recovery: RECOVERY.txt.
  Resource interruptions and local/CI validation outcomes are preserved in
  resource-notes.json, validation-resource-hold.json, and ci-notes.json.

No playable bot weights were changed or automatically promoted by this study.
