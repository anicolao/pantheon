Three difficulty tiers, 2026-10-30.

Standard: current production actors from pinned main.
Hard: selected best-field actors, all planning disabled.
Expert: identical actors to Hard, with selected Doreios/Thaleia/Melia planners.
Melia is identical between Standard and Hard; Nereon is identical at all levels.
Difficulty separation is therefore aggregate, not guaranteed for each leader.

Two bots from each tier in every game; all six leader assignments balanced over
120 seed blocks, four seat rotations each. Run Hard/Standard and Expert/Hard;
reuse completed Expert/Standard on exactly the same 480-game schedule. Scores
are individual wins with split tie credit, aggregated across the two bots in
each tier; these bots do not cooperate as teams. Confidence intervals cluster
four rotations per seed block. No parameter tuning or outcome exclusions.

Inputs are copied under inputs/; protocol.json pins checksums. Each game's
full-state replay is verified. Script: scripts/rl/compare_difficulty_tiers.py.
