# Wealth, supply, and repeatable turns

This implements [step 5](../../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) against the accepted [Treasure](../../../docs/ux/concepts/05-treasures.png), [supply](../../../docs/ux/concepts/06-supply.png), [inspection](../../../docs/ux/concepts/07-card-detail.png), and [departure decision](../../../docs/ux/concepts/21-end-turn.png) concepts. Cleanup and turn handoff are brought forward so a player can reach newly purchased Actions through ordinary play.

The table retains its real hand, leader, altars and counters. Only the hovered hand card glows; keyboard focus remains visible. A tap or click plays directly, while right-click or long-press opens inspection. Treasure mode begins automatically when there are no Actions left to spend or no playable Actions in hand, after pending choices finish. The last card result remains visible in the status line and Chronicle even though the phase transition is recorded.

Supply is a single cost-sorted coverflow with two face-up cards on phones, three on desktop and four on large tables, plus slanted neighboring cards. At the cheapest boundary the face-up group can contain fewer cards. The most expensive affordable pile starts at the right edge of the face-up group. Swipe, wheel and arrow controls browse the full supply. Tapping a card buys it directly; a framed warning appears only when playable Actions remain. Unavailable and empty piles can still be inspected by holding or right-clicking. Live stock and the discard count remain visible. Unavailable faces darken without becoming translucent. On desktop and 4K, a purchased card travels from its pile to the discard indicator once; reconnecting does not replay it. No substitute assets, tolerance exceptions or masks are used.
