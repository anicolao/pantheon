To Treasures and End turn occupy the same reachable position. Supply accepts purchases directly after the Action phase; the first purchase atomically closes Treasure play, removing the separate To Buys click. The turn marker opens Chronicle. Reminders name the highest-cost legal card (card ID breaks cost ties), rather than the first hand card or cheapest supply pile.

Supply is a full-screen table scene with six inset piles per page, live stock, Basics/Actions selectors, selected-card glow, an actual large card, cost and Buy icons, a distinct Buy control, discard destination and a near-hand strip. Mobile places the six piles in two rows while preserving the selected card and cost below. Cards retain the approved full faces rather than the paintings’ abbreviated faces. Every selected card has full-sized inspection, even when empty or unaffordable. The actual gained copy travels to discard on desktop; the mobile destination count and public table result convey the same outcome with less space. No substitute assets, tolerance exceptions or masks are used.
