# Wealth, supply, and repeatable turns

This implements [step 5](../../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) against the accepted [Treasure](../../../docs/ux/concepts/05-treasures.png), [supply](../../../docs/ux/concepts/06-supply.png), [inspection](../../../docs/ux/concepts/07-card-detail.png), and [departure decision](../../../docs/ux/concepts/21-end-turn.png) concepts. Cleanup and turn handoff are brought forward so a player can reach newly purchased Actions through ordinary play.

The table retains its real hand, leader, altars and counters. Playable Treasures glow, inspection offers individual play, and an explicit Play all Treasures control commits the whole set atomically. To Treasures, To Buys and End turn occupy the same reachable position. The turn marker opens Chronicle. A player can retain Treasures; phase changes are forward-only, and only a remaining legal option prompts a decision.

Supply is a full-screen table scene with six inset piles per page, live stock, Basics/Actions selectors, selected-card glow, an actual large card, cost and Buy icons, a distinct Buy control, discard destination and a near-hand strip. Mobile places the six piles in two rows while preserving the selected card and cost below. Cards retain the approved full faces rather than the paintings’ abbreviated faces. Every selected card has full-sized inspection, even when empty or unaffordable. The actual gained copy travels to discard on desktop; the mobile destination count and public table result convey the same outcome with less space. No substitute assets, tolerance exceptions or masks are used.

There is no injected history in this story. It checks live observer visibility, one-time purchase animation, lost acknowledgement, preserved copy identity and restoration. Additional stories cover multiple Buys, retained Treasures, cost-zero payment, unavailable controls, empty-pile inspection, cancelling departure and explicit handoff. Empty-pile preparation uses a complete legal recorded match.

Captures wait for transaction acknowledgement as well as fonts, images and finite animation.
