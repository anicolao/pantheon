# Mobile visual review

The original phone Chronicle stretched a 3:2 painting into a tall dialog. User review correctly identified the distorted crest and laurels. The phone frame now preserves their proportions. The Leave Actions and End turn panels had the same issue and use the same approach. No new raster artwork was generated.

The review covered the 421 existing phone captures: gathering/invitations/recovery, Sanctuary, bloodline drafting for 2–4 players, the active and observing table, private card inspection, Actions and choices, supply/purchases, all gods and Worship choices, turn handoff, all match endings and score inspection, public history/piles, gallery, and rules. Changed captures were reviewed together, with representative frames inspected at full resolution.

At the standard 393 × 852 phone viewport, the complete phone suite exercises all those flows. Additional recorded-history journeys exercise the Chronicle, portrait and landscape card inspection, In play, empty discard, shared trash, and return to the two-player table at the sizes below. The recorded prelude uses real legal commands and is not represented as UI play.

Short-phone layouts remove the table’s old 700px minimum height, keep the turn rail clear of the hand, and keep the opponent’s portrait and public counts below the header. The tray keeps 44px interactive controls, compacts resource totals, and omits disabled effect-paging controls when the entire move fits on one page. Public cards preserve their portrait/landscape formats and open for full inspection.

All captures retain the clipping and control-overlap audits, exact-pixel comparison, and the original two-second operation and capture budgets. The broad review covers portrait phone views; it is not a claim that every device/orientation has been tested.
