Study
-----
Historical book search: 10,428 games. Current Engine search: 9,888 games.
Screening and refinement are separate; every affordable card and pass are candidates at represented opening observations.
Books condition on own turn and observed coins, never future hands. Turn one is frozen before optimizing turn two.
This is a small sequential search against Money, not an exhaustive joint opening tree or proof of optimality.
Held-out matrix: 108 legal ordered cells, 200 matched fresh seeds each, 21,600 games total.
Held-out opening controls: 48 cells, 100 matched seeds each, 4,800 games total.
No books were retuned using held-out results. No strategies or leaders were averaged into a balance score.

Reading the results
-------------------
matrix-report.txt: full 12×12 P1/P2 matrix and strongest observed P2 replies for each fixed P1 choice.
matrix-analysis.json: per-cell counts and pointwise 95% intervals.
pairwise-summary.txt: P1's best observed worst-case strategy and P2's reply for each ordered leader pairing, using actual cells rather than averages.
seat-comparisons.txt: paired opposite-seat differences and 95% intervals.
opening-control-report.txt: all 48 book-minus-baseline comparisons, separately by Engine, leader, rival, and seat.

With its selected book, historical Engine is Thaleia's strongest tested P1 choice against each rival's best reply:
43.75% against Nereon Money, 46.50% against Melia Money, and 61.50% against Doreios Money.
Doreios remains weak as P1 even with its best tested choice, historical Engine:
31.00% against Thaleia Money, 27.25% against Nereon Money, and 17.75% against Melia Money.
Money has large seat effects in several pairings. For Thaleia Money versus Nereon Money, Thaleia scores 19.25% as P1 and 56.00% as P2; paired P2-minus-P1 is +36.75 points [28.67, 44.83].
These seat comparisons include bot behavior, not just the equal-turn rule, and should not be interpreted as a causal rule-change test.

Opening-book controls are mixed. The following are all comparisons whose pointwise paired 95% interval excludes zero (100 games each):
- Nereon current Engine vs Thaleia Money, P1: 31.0% -> 45.0%, +14.0 points [3.3, 24.7].
- Nereon current Engine vs Thaleia Money, P2: 36.5% -> 60.5%, +24.0 points [14.9, 33.1].
- Melia current Engine vs Doreios Money, P2: 63.0% -> 72.5%, +9.5 points [3.4, 15.6].
- Melia historical Engine vs Doreios Money, P2: 62.0% -> 77.5%, +15.5 points [5.5, 25.5].
- Nereon historical Engine vs Doreios Money, P2: 61.5% -> 78.5%, +17.0 points [7.5, 26.5].
- Thaleia current Engine vs Melia Money, P1: 32.5% -> 26.5%, -6.0 points [-10.9, -1.1].
All other control intervals include zero. These are pointwise intervals, not simultaneous multiple-comparison-adjusted conclusions.
The installed books remain the frozen selections used in the matrix; the study does not establish universal improvement.
Zero observed paired differences do not establish population equivalence.
Best-response selections are descriptive and use the same data as their cell intervals; they are not independently validated equilibrium policies.
These results assess the tested bots and do not establish optimal human-play balance.
