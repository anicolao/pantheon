Add local practice opponents and independent leader opening books

Players can try current Money, current Engine, and historical v4 Engine locally at /practice/, choose either seat and distinct leaders, pause/resume, restore a saved game, and export its replay. Decisions use only legal observations in a dedicated worker. Practice uses the study's equal-turn rule and original ties; regular multiplayer ending behavior is unchanged.

Each Engine now has independently selected books for all four leaders. The fresh 12×12 matrix contains 21,600 games; 4,800 paired controls separately measure the books against unchanged Money. The books have mixed effects: Nereon's current Engine improves against Thaleia Money in both seats, while Thaleia's current Engine declines against Melia Money as P1. Books were frozen before held-out evaluation and were not retuned from these results.
