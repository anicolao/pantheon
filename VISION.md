# Pantheon: Bloodlines — Vision

## The promise

Lead a mythic Greek bloodline from humble beginnings to a flourishing empire. Build a deck that expresses your leader’s identity, court the gods, and claim territory before your rivals secure the most valuable lands.

## The experience

Players should feel their power grow through deliberate choices. Small purchases become reliable combinations, those combinations become prosperity, and prosperity becomes an empire. Victory should depend on recognizing when further preparation is less valuable than claiming land.

Divine affiliation should give those choices character. Wisdom, the sea, the harvest, and martial ambition should suggest distinct paths to power. The mythology should shape how an empire develops, rather than serving only as decoration.

## Design pillars

- **An empire built through its deck.** Development and territory compete for space in the same evolving engine, creating meaningful tension between future strength and present victory.
- **Distinct leaders, open possibilities.** A bloodline offers a recognizable identity and an early advantage, while leaving room to combine affiliations or change direction.
- **A shared divine presence.** The selected leaders bring their gods into the game. Those gods become opportunities for everyone, so each leader choice helps shape the shared contest.
- **Readable depth.** A clear turn structure and understandable card effects support combinations that reward planning and discovery.
- **A visible race.** Players can see valuable lands disappearing and opponents gathering strength. Timing and competition for shared opportunities drive the tension.

## Direction

Establish a satisfying core of acquisition, affiliation, and territorial growth before expanding the world. New content should deepen strategic expression and mythic identity while preserving a game players can understand from the cards and a compact ruleset.

Success means players can explain what made their bloodline distinctive, point to a consequential choice about development versus territory, and want to try a different route to empire in the next game.
